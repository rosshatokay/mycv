# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: July 30, 2026**

---

### 1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly provided by you to display your public Profile and maintain your account:

* **Account Information:** Email address, username, and password or authentication details.
* **Profile Data:** Full name, current role, work experience, education, personal projects, and social media/contact links.
* **Uploaded Media:** Profile images and project media uploaded to your page.

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

* Generate, host, and display your public profile page.
* Manage your user account and authenticate logins.
* Respond to customer support or feature requests.

### 3. Data Visibility & Sharing

* **Public Profile:** The professional details and media you publish on your Profile page are publicly visible to anyone who visits your URL.
* **Third-Party Service Providers:** We may use third-party vendors (such as cloud hosting and database storage providers) to run the Service infrastructure. These providers process your data strictly to facilitate the application's functionality.
* **No Selling of Data:** We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties or advertising networks.

### 4. Data Retention & Deletion

We store your data for as long as your account remains active. You may edit or delete your Profile information at any time via your account settings. If you wish to permanently delete your account and associated data, please contact us.

### 5. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential session storage/cookies required for authenticating your session and keeping you logged in. We do not use third-party tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

### 6. Security

We implement reasonable technical measures to secure your data against unauthorized access. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

### 7. Contact

If you have any questions regarding these terms or your privacy, please reach out through the contact options provided on our site.
